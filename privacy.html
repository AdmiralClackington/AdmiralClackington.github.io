Higgins privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-09.

Who uses Higgins

Higgins is a personal assistant used only by Admiral Clackington, on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With Admiral Clackington's permission, given through Google sign-in, Higgins requests these Google permissions:

  • Gmail: read messages, create drafts and manage labels (gmail.readonly, gmail.compose, gmail.modify, gmail.labels). Higgins does not send email.
  • Google Calendar: read and manage events (calendar, calendar.events).
  • Google Drive: read files (drive.readonly).

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer Admiral Clackington's own requests: finding and summarising email, drafting replies for their review, and reading and arranging their calendar and documents.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS keychain on Admiral Clackington's computer.
  • Google data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • To answer a request, the relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider that Admiral Clackington has chosen for their assistant. It is processed only to produce that answer.
  • Admiral Clackington can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Higgins's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
